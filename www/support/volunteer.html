Volunteer

Volunteer roles are available across TDHHC programs. Positions working directly with Deaf and DeafBlind community members require ASL fluency and Deaf cultural knowledge.

Some volunteer roles require minimal sign language. TDHHC posts on this site when volunteer applications open.

Email coordinator@tdhhc.org to express interest.
