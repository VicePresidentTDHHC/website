About TDHHC

The Tyler Deaf and Hard of Hearing Center is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2012 by members of the local Deaf community.

Mission

TDHHC provides comprehensive services and resources for Deaf, Hard of Hearing, and DeafBlind people across East Texas — including interpreter coordination, advocacy, education, and community connection from our Tyler office.

Service area

Based in Tyler, TDHHC serves individuals and organizations across the East Texas region. Programs include interpreter services, ASL classes, senior and youth activities, literacy initiatives, and community events.
