				<div class="et_pb_text_inner"><h3 class="p2">Our interpreter services have become well established in the community, offering much needed services for deaf individuals. Our interpreters are certified and qualified to provide excellent service in a variety of settings.</h3>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><b>The Tyler Deaf and Hard of Hearing Center</b> (TDHHC) makes it easier for entities like doctor <span class="s2">’</span>s offices, hospitals and attorneys to comply with ADA regulations that prohibit exclusivity by providing a simple agreement to ensure we consistently and reliably offer services at predictable, straight forward rates.<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span></p>
<p class="p2">Our prices are reasonable and competitive. For example, the base rate for community/medical interpreting is the same whether the assignment<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span>requires a basic level or a master level interpreter. This allows TDHHC provide the client (you) with the most appropriate and effective interpreter regardless of the assignment. In other words, if you need a master level interpreter, it won<span class="s2">’</span>t cost you anymore than if you needed a basic level interpreter, but you can always feel confident that you will receive an interpreter fit to your assignment’s specific needs.<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span></p>
<p class="p2">Specialty interpreters, such as Court/Legal and the like, have a separate but still reasonable, and industry competitive billing rate. We are a non-profit organization. Our goal is not to make money, but to pay great interpreters an industry standard wage to insure we always have the best interpreters available for our clients.<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span></p>
<p class="p2">Additionally, businesses and other entities can confidently offer their deaf consumers qualified certified interpreters without extra expense based on the interpreter<span class="s2">’</span>s level of certification. This allows TDHHC to be any business entity<span class="s2">’</span>s <span class="s2">&#8221; insurance &#8221; </span>or <span class="s2">“</span>back-up plan” even if they use another agencies’ services.<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span></p></div>
			</div><div class="et_pb_module et_pb_text et_pb_text_48  et_pb_text_align_left et_pb_bg_layout_light">
				
				
				
				
				<div class="et_pb_text_inner"><p class="p2"><strong>INTERPRETERS</strong></p>
<p class="p2">If you are interested in becoming one of our interpreters submit your resume here and our Interpreter Coordinator will be in touch with you. Also, check our job postings page for information on potential job availabilities.</p>
<p><strong>INTERPRETER TRAINING PROGRAMS </strong></p>
<p class="p1">For information about how to become an interpreter, contact TJC Sign Language Interpreter Training Program: <a href="http://www.tjc.edu/signlanguage"><span class="s1">www.tjc.edu/signlanguage</span></a>.<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span></p>
<p class="p1">We understand the importance of growing advanced interpreters; TDHHC provides mentoring opportunities for interpreters who wish to attain higher/specialized certifications.<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span></p>
<p class="p1">Shadowing opportunities are available for student interpreters.<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span></p>
<p class="p1">The Tyler Deaf and Hard of Hearing Center strives to be an all-inclusive deaf-friendly resource. We hope you enjoy and make use of our helpful information, resources, and services.<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span></p>
