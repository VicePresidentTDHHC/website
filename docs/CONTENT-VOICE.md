# TDHHC Content Voice & Tone

**Constraint for the entire redesign:** No patronizing language. Write as a community resource speaking to peers — not as a charity speaking down to recipients.

Apply this guide to all new copy, all migrated copy, form labels, buttons, CTAs, and alt text.

---

## Core principle

TDHHC serves Deaf, Hard of Hearing, and DeafBlind people as **equals**. The site should sound:

- **Direct** — say what you do, how to access it
- **Factual** — policies, rates, hours, steps
- **Respectful** — no pity, no inspiration porn, no filler warmth

Avoid the tone of a customer-service chatbot or a fundraising appeal when talking to community members seeking services.

---

## Do not use

Phrases like these appear on the current site. **Do not carry them forward.**

| Patronizing / filler | Why it fails | Example from archive |
|----------------------|--------------|----------------------|
| "We're here to help" | Empty; implies visitor is helpless | Interpreter services |
| "We're here for you!" | Same | Contact page |
| "Have more questions? Not a problem." | Dismissive filler before an action | Interpreter services |
| "Let us know how we can help" | Vague; centers TDHHC as helper | Contact page |
| "We look forward to helping you" | Performative | Home contact card |
| "We hope you enjoy…" | Treats resources like a gift basket | Home, interpreter footer |
| "Curious about what kinds of services…" | Condescending opener | Home services card |
| "Click here" (alone) | Non-descriptive; talks down | Multiple pages |
| "Life blood" / "generous donation" | Emotional manipulation | Donate page |
| "Passionate leaders… voices are heard" | Inspiration cliché | Board page |
| "Please let us know what we can help you with" | Patronizing form placeholder | Contact form |
| "One-stop shop" (without context) | Marketing jargon; vague | Home hero |
| "Helpful resources" (repeated) | Self-congratulatory filler | Resources intro |
| "Thank you for considering" (excessive) | Groveling tone | Donate page |

Also avoid:
- **"Suffer from"** hearing loss — use "are Deaf/Hard of Hearing/DeafBlind" or "have hearing loss" per context
- **"Despite their disability"** — never
- **"Special needs"** — use "accommodation," "access," or name the specific need
- **Exclamation points** in service/contact copy (save for genuine announcements if needed)
- **"Simply" / "just" / "easy"** — minimizes real barriers people face

## Use instead

| Situation | Instead of… | Write… |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Contact intro | "We're here for you!" | "Contact TDHHC by VP, voice, email, or the form below." |
| More questions | "Have more questions? We're here to help." | "Questions: contact Andrea Short" + VP, voice, email links |
| Services intro | "Curious about what kinds of services…" | "TDHHC provides interpreter services, advocacy, and community programs in East Texas." |
| Home mission | "We hope you enjoy our helpful resources" | "TDHHC connects East Texas Deaf, Hard of Hearing, and DeafBlind communities to services, advocacy, and resources." |
| Donate | "Life blood… generous donation… thank you once again" | State 501(c)(3) status, what donations fund, how to give — once, plainly |
| Board intro | "Passionate leaders… voices are heard" | Factual: who governs TDHHC, what the board does |
| Form label | "What we can help you with" | "Your message" or "Reason for contact" |
| CTA link | "Click here" | Descriptive: "Contact TDHHC," "Request an interpreter," "View resources" |
| Interpreter page | "Not a problem" | Omit — go straight to contact info or FAQ |

## Audience-specific tone

### Service seekers (Deaf, HoH, DeafBlind community)

- Peer-to-peer, not provider-to-patient
- Name **access** and **rights** (ADA, effective communication) — not charity
- VP number is a first-class contact method, not an afterthought
- ASL content is primary where possible; written English supports it

### Businesses and organizations

- Professional, compliance-oriented
- Plain language about ADA obligations and booking process
- No guilt; state facts: entities request interpreters, entities pay, rates, billing cycle

### Supporters (donors, volunteers)

- Honest about need without emotional manipulation
- Specific about what money and time fund
- Respect donor intelligence — no excessive thanks layered on thanks

## Identity and terminology

| Term | Usage |
|------|--------|
| Deaf | Capital D when referring to cultural/linguistic community |
| Hard of Hearing | Capital H in H when part of community name |
| DeafBlind | One word, capital D and B |
| "the deaf and hard of hearing" (lowercase) | Avoid in new copy — use community names above or "Deaf and Hard of Hearing communities" |
| Hearing loss | Acceptable when describing condition, not identity |
| Interpreter | Not "translator" for ASL |
| Video Phone (VP) | Spell out on first use per page if needed |

Staff may have preferences — confirm once and document here.

## Rewrite reference (archive → redesign)

Ready replacements for known site copy:

**Contact page**
- ~~Let us know how we can help. Call, email, fill out our form, drop us a note. We're here for you! Thank you.~~
- **Contact TDHHC by video phone, voice, email, or the form below.**

**Interpreter services**
- ~~Have more questions? Not a problem. We're here to help.~~
- **Questions:** Andrea Short, Interpreter Coordinator — [VP] · [voice] · coordinator@tdhhc.org

**Home — contact card**
- ~~We look forward to helping you.~~
- **Contact TDHHC** (link) — programs, donations, volunteer opportunities

**Home — services card**
- ~~Curious about what kinds of services TDHHC offers?~~
- **TDHHC offers interpreter services, advocacy, literacy programs, and community resources.**

**Home — mission band**
- ~~We hope you enjoy and make use of our helpful information, resources, and services.~~
- **A community resource center for Deaf, Hard of Hearing, and DeafBlind people in East Texas.**

**Donate**
- ~~They truly are the 'life blood'… We'd like to thank you once again for your generous donation.~~
- **TDHHC is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations fund interpreter services, advocacy, and community programs.**

**Board**
- ~~Passionate group of leaders… shape a world where voices are heard.~~
- **The TDHHC Board of Directors sets policy and direction for the organization.**

**Form placeholder**
- ~~please let us know what we can help you with~~
- **Your message**

---

## Review checklist (before any page ships)

- [ ] No "here to help," "here for you," or equivalent filler
- [ ] CTAs use descriptive link text (not "click here")
- [ ] No pity framing or inspiration clichés
- [ ] VP and voice contact methods named equally
- [ ] Form labels are neutral and specific
- [ ] Donate copy states facts, not emotional appeals
- [ ] Read aloud: would a Deaf community member find this respectful?
