For businesses: ADA & billing

TDHHC helps doctor's offices, hospitals, attorneys, and other entities comply with ADA requirements for effective communication. Community and medical assignments use the same base rate whether a basic- or master-level interpreter is assigned. Specialty assignments (court/legal, etc.) use a separate published rate.

You may request or decline a specific interpreter when scheduling. Interpreters cannot be double-booked; if your preferred interpreter is unavailable, the coordinator will contact you.

FAQ

Who pays for interpreter services?

The business or organization that requests the service is responsible for payment. TDHHC bills monthly.

Can a Deaf individual request an interpreter directly?

Requests come from the entity providing the appointment (clinic, court, employer, etc.). TDHHC can help individuals understand how to request access from that entity.
